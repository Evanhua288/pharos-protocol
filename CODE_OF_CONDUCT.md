# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists as a public instrument. Participation is judged on the
quality and verifiability of contributions, not on the identity, seniority,
employer, or commercial position of the contributor.

Expected:
- Argue from the specification and from reproducible evidence.
- Separate what is verifiable from what is asserted.
- State uncertainty plainly; do not present a claim as established fact.
- Accept being corrected by an independent reproduction.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Presenting an assertion as a verified result.
- Removing or weakening a judgement criterion in order to pass it.
- Silently changing a published vector or proof.
- Using this project's name to imply certification, endorsement, or approval
  by any party.

## Scope

Applies to repositories, issue trackers, review comments, and any channel
where someone speaks on behalf of this project.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainers at **evanhua288@gmail.com** (interim address, replaceable). Reports are handled in confidence. Consequences range from a
private warning to permanent exclusion, depending on severity and pattern.

## On disagreement

Disagreement about substance is welcome and expected. Where two
implementations disagree, the tie-breaker is a reproducible test vector and
the published rule — never the identity of whoever speaks loudest.
